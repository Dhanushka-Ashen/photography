About The Artist

“Photography is the art of pausing a heartbeat forever.”

Hello! I am Dhanushka Ashen, a passionate professional photographer based in Sri Lanka, available islandwide and globally. For me, photography isn't just about staging poses—it is about feeling the raw pulse of genuine laughter, unspoken tears of joy, and the silent poetry between two souls.

Whether framing a grand traditional wedding, an intimate sunset pre-shoot by the southern coast, or an empowering editorial portrait, my vision blends cinematic natural lighting with relaxed, documentary-style storytelling.

No stiff posing. Relaxed direction that brings out your true personality.
